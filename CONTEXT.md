# HPHI Website

Public website and CMS for 專業心理治療及催眠應用（香港）有限公司 (HPHI), a Hong Kong psychotherapy and hypnosis institute. Editors manage content in the admin; visitors browse courses, tutors and posts.

## Language

### Site chrome

**Header**:
The bar pinned to the top of the viewport on every page, holding the logo, the main menu and search.
_Avoid_: Navbar (when meaning the whole bar)

**Announcement（公告列）**:
A single editor-written message shown at the top of the Header, which visitors can dismiss.
_Avoid_: Banner (that is a rich-text block), notice

**Social Strip（社交條）**:
A band at the very top of every page, above the Header, presenting the institute's Social Channels; it scrolls away with the page instead of staying pinned, and visitors can dismiss it for the rest of their visit. Not part of the Header, even when the client calls it "header".
_Avoid_: QR header, QR bar, top bar

**Social Channel（社交渠道）**:
One of the institute's public accounts on an external platform (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp) through which visitors can follow or contact it, presented as a QR code, a link and a Caption.
_Avoid_: Social link, platform, QR

**Caption（說明）**:
Free-form text accompanying a Social Channel; editors decide its language(s) and line breaks.
